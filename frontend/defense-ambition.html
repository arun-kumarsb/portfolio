Defense & Service — A Long-Term Ambition

Inspired by service. Driven by technology. Exploring where engineering and national service could take me.

My interest in defense is personal. My father's service gave me an early connection to the armed forces and shaped my respect for the idea of serving the country. As an engineering student, I have found my own path toward that interest through technology, software engineering, and systems thinking.

One of my long-term goals is to explore becoming an Army Officer, including opportunities such as CDS or AFCAT if I meet the relevant eligibility requirements. I know that this is a competitive path, and I see it as an aspiration that I need to work toward rather than something I can assume will happen.

At the same time, I want to develop into a strong software engineer with knowledge of backend systems, distributed architectures, AI/ML, cybersecurity, and communication systems. Whether my future contribution comes through military service, defense technology, or another engineering role, I want the skills I build to have a meaningful purpose.
